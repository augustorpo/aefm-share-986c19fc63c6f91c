Duolingo Q3 2026: Users Surge, EPS Slips, Stock Pops

Nov 3, 2026 · 8:56

Duolingo's Q3 2026: revenue $309.0M (+14%) beat the ~$303M consensus, adjusted EPS $0.54 missed the $0.57 estimate, daily active users accelerated to 64.9M (+29%), and full-year guidance went up. The stock fell 5.4% in the regular session to $128, then jumped about 11% after hours. Max (bull) and Lena (skeptic) debate the user comeback, bookings vs revenue, guidance, and the cracks: lower EBITDA and free cash flow, a DAU bonus accrual, and a gross margin guided down.
